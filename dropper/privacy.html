Dropper privacy policy

Last updated: 12 November 2026

Dropper is a game for Android made by Dot.Games_Dev ("we"). This page explains what data the game and the Google services built into it handle, and why.

In short

  • The game has no accounts and no servers of its own. Your progress is stored on your phone.
  • Ads are served by Google AdMob, which collects data to show and measure ads. Where the law requires it, the game asks for your choices before any ad is shown.
  • Purchases go through Google Play. We never see your payment details.
  • If you sign in to Google Play Games, your progress is also saved in your Google account so it can sync between your phones.
  • We don't use analytics or tracking of our own.

What the game stores on your phone

Your progress (keys, realms, upgrades, colors, achievements, your collection and the run in progress) and your settings, such as sound and vibration. The game also creates a random ID when it's installed. It's used only to merge cloud saves from several phones, and isn't linked to your phone's hardware or advertising ID.

This data stays on your phone, except as described below. Android may include it in your phone's own backup to your Google account, which you control in your phone's settings. Uninstalling the game deletes it from the phone.

Cloud save (Google Play Games)

If you sign in to Google Play Games from the game, your progress is saved as a Play Games saved game in your Google account and read back on your other phones. Google stores it under the Google Privacy Policy. Signing in is optional. You can delete the saved data in your Google Play Games settings.

Purchases (Google Play)

Key packs are sold through Google Play, which handles the payment. The game receives only a confirmation of which pack you bought, so it can give you the keys once. Google shares order details with us, such as the order number, the product, the price and your country, which we use only for refunds, support and accounting.

Ads (Google AdMob)

The game shows rewarded ads that you choose to watch, for example to continue a run. They are served by Google AdMob through the Google Mobile Ads SDK, which may collect and process:

  • your phone's advertising ID;
  • your IP address, and an approximate location worked out from it;
  • information about your phone and the app, such as the model, Android version and language;
  • how you interact with ads;
  • diagnostics, such as crash and performance data from the SDK.

Google uses this to show and measure ads, to personalize them where you have allowed it, and to prevent fraud. Data is sent to Google over encrypted connections. Read how Google uses it: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

In the European Economic Area, the UK, Switzerland and US states with privacy laws, the game shows Google's consent form before ads, and you can change your choices at any time with AD PRIVACY CHOICES on the game's pause screen. On any phone, you can reset or delete your advertising ID in Android's settings, under Privacy → Ads.

Children

Dropper isn't directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal data from them.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct or delete personal data, or object to how it's used. We hold no personal data from the game other than the order details above. For data held by Google, use your Google Account's data and privacy settings. For anything else, contact us below.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date at the top.
